# Code of Conduct

Xonava adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions,
or any other project space, is expected to follow it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on technical grounds.
- Acknowledge the work of others and credit contributions accurately.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

To report an incident, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub at <https://github.com/xonava>.
Do not report conduct concerns in public issues or pull requests. Maintainers will review each report
and respond as the Contributor Covenant describes.

Security vulnerabilities are a separate matter and should be reported as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
